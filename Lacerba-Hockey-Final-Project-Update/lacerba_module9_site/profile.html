Description

Goaltending is an important position in ice hockey. The goalie protects the net and tries to stop the other team from scoring. A goalie needs concentration, quick reflexes, confidence, and the ability to follow the puck while working together with teammates.

Equipment

Goalie equipment includes a mask, chest protector, catching glove, blocker, leg pads, goalie stick, skates, and protective clothing.

Skills to Practice

  • Positioning: Stay square to the puck and cover shooting angles.
  • Rebound control: Direct the puck away from the front of the net.
  • Communication: Talk with teammates and organize the defense.
