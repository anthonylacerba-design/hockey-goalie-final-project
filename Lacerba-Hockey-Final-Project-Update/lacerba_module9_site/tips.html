Beginner's Guide to Being a Goalie

What the goalie does, the basics to learn first, and tips for getting started.

The Goalie's Job

A hockey goalie is the last line of defense. The main job is to stop the puck from entering the net, track the play, control rebounds, and communicate with teammates.

New goalies should focus on safe equipment, a balanced stance, watching the puck, and practicing one skill at a time with a coach.

Beginner Goalie Tips

  • Use a ready stance: knees bent, chest up, hands in front.
  • Keep the stick blade on the ice for low shots.
  • Track the puck with your eyes and stay square to it.
  • Learn butterfly technique gradually with a coach.
  • Wear properly fitted goalie equipment and warm up.
  • Reset mentally after every shot, save, or goal.

Practice Plan

  1. Start with stance and balance.
  2. Practice controlled movement and getting set.
  3. Track soft shots from different angles.
  4. Work on glove and blocker saves.
  5. Direct rebounds away from danger.
  6. Ask a coach for feedback and build confidence.
