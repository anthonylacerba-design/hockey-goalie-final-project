Website Purpose and Audience

Purpose

The purpose of my website is to create a personal hockey goalie website where I can share my experience, interests, and knowledge about playing goalie. The site provides a place for visitors to learn about my hockey journey and find goalie tips, profile information, photos, and a video resource. It organizes my hockey content in one place and builds a personal online presence around the sport.

Audience

My main audience is hockey players, especially people who are interested in becoming a goalie or improving their goalie skills. The website is also intended for younger players, families, teammates, and other hockey fans. The responsive layout is designed to be easy to use on phones, tablets, and computers.

Website Structure

The Home page introduces the website. About Me shares my hockey background and experience. Goalie Profile presents facts about the position, equipment, and skills. Goalie Tips provides beginner guidance and a YouTube video. Live Feed displays hockey photos and goalie development information. This final project combines the content and skills developed in earlier modules into one responsive Bootstrap website.
